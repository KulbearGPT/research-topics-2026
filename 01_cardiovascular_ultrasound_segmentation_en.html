Social Impact 01 · Medical AI

Temporal Consistency and Uncertainty-aware Segmentation for Cardiovascular Ultrasound Videos

Build an automatic segmentation framework for cardiovascular ultrasound images and videos, improving single-frame accuracy, cross-frame stability, weak-label robustness, and the reliability of EF / EDV / ESV estimation.

Input: ultrasound image / video sequenceMiddle: temporal alignment / uncertainty mapOutput: stable masks + clinical metrics

Medical image segmentation should not only make a single-frame mask look accurate; it should be stable over time and reliable enough to support metric estimation.

Research Framing

Medical AI through stability and reliability

This direction examines how deep learning methods connect with real health-related problems. It offers clear visual results and a strong social-impact story through clinical metrics and generalization tests.

Research question

Why does high single-frame Dice not guarantee reliable ultrasound video analysis?

Analyze how speckle noise, blurred boundaries, and fast cardiac motion create mask jitter that affects volume and EF estimation.

Contribution

Temporal consistency + uncertainty-aware learning

Design cross-frame alignment, uncertainty-based pseudo-label filtering, and clinical consistency constraints, then validate each module through ablations.

Possible outputs

Research artifacts

Baseline reproduction, video segmentation demo, uncertainty maps, temporal smoothness curves, cross-dataset tests, and a conference-style paper.

Research Position

Core problem

Many segmentation models process each frame independently, but cardiac structures move continuously through the cardiac cycle.

This project shifts the goal from “is the mask accurate?” to “is the mask stable, trustworthy, and useful for metric computation?”

Research Path

Research path

Progress is organized by research maturity and concrete intermediate artifacts.

Stage ATask and data

Understand ultrasound views, LV / myocardium / LA labels, EF / EDV / ESV, and data splits.

Stage BSegmentation baseline

Reproduce U-Net, DeepLabV3+, TransUNet, or Swin-UNet.

Stage CTemporal consistency

Add ConvLSTM, cross-frame attention, or temporal loss to reduce mask jitter.

Stage DUncertainty and metric constraints

Use uncertainty maps, pseudo-label filtering, and EF / volume consistency constraints.

Stage EPaper experiments

Complete comparisons, ablations, cross-dataset tests, metric analysis, and failure cases.

Research Details

Core technical components

The method focuses on video modeling, uncertainty estimation, and clinical consistency.

Temporal Feature Fusion

Align or attend across neighboring frames so the model understands continuous cardiac motion.

Uncertainty-aware Decoder

Predict both masks and uncertainty to identify low-confidence boundaries and weak pseudo-labels.

Clinical Consistency

Connect segmentation outputs with EF, EDV, ESV, or cardiac phase constraints.
