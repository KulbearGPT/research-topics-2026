Social Impact 03 · Urban Risk AI

Remote Sensing and Urban Graph Fusion for Flood Risk Prediction

Fuse SAR, optical remote sensing, DEM, rainfall, road networks, building footprints, and land-cover information to predict flooded areas, flooded roads, flooded buildings, and grid-level urban flood risk.

Input: remote sensing + rainfall + DEM + urban graphMiddle: remote sensing encoder + GNN fusionOutput: flood mask + road / building risk

Urban flood prediction should not only answer where water is, but also which roads, buildings, and infrastructure are affected and why risk concentrates there.

Student Research View

Urban computing with disaster-response value

This topic is suitable for students interested in remote sensing, urban data, graph neural networks, and public-safety problems. It goes beyond generic water segmentation by modeling urban topology and object-level risk.

Research question

Why is urban flooding not just ordinary water segmentation?

Analyze how building occlusion, road topology, elevation, rainfall, and impervious surfaces jointly shape flood distribution and affected objects.

Contribution

Remote sensing + urban graph fusion

Combine remote-sensing segmentation features with road, building, or grid graph structures to predict both pixel-level flooding and object-level risk.

Expected output

Disaster-risk analysis loop

Flood segmentation, flooded-road and flooded-building detection, cross-city tests, risk-factor explanation maps, and a conference-style paper.

Research Position

Core problem

Dense urban buildings can obscure flood signals in SAR and optical imagery, and urban flooding is often small, sparse, and highly imbalanced.

This project moves from detecting water to identifying affected infrastructure and explaining risk sources.

Research Path

Research path

Each stage has a concrete intermediate artifact.

Stage ATask and data

Understand SAR / optical flood labels, road and building object labels, DEM, rainfall, and spatial alignment.

Stage BSegmentation baseline

Reproduce U-Net, DeepLabV3+, SegFormer, or Swin-UNet for flood segmentation.

Stage CMulti-modal fusion

Compare SAR-only, optical-only, SAR+optical, and hydrological-variable fusion.

Stage DUrban graph modeling

Add road and building graph structure to predict object-level flooded probabilities.

Stage EGeneralization and explanation

Test across events, cities, and small-object areas; produce risk-factor explanation maps.

Research Details

Core technical components

The method centers on remote-sensing encoding, urban graph modeling, hydrological variables, and object-level evaluation.

Remote Sensing Encoder

Encode SAR, optical, or multi-temporal imagery to extract pixel-level flood features.

Urban Graph Encoder

Represent roads, buildings, or grids as graph nodes and model spatial topology with GNNs.

Cross-modal Risk Decoder

Fuse remote sensing, graph structure, rainfall, and terrain variables to predict pixel-level and object-level risk.
