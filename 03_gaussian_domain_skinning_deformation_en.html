Topic 03 · Deformation

Gaussian-domain Skinning and Deformation

Study how a rigged Gaussian asset should update its position, orientation, and scale under a target pose so animation rendering remains stable.

Input: rest Gaussian + skeleton + poseMiddle: center / frame / scale updateOutput: animated Gaussian frames

A Gaussian primitive is more than a point: it has orientation, scale, opacity, and appearance. Moving only the center can create stretching, blur, and joint artifacts.

Student Research View

Advanced graphics research challenge

This topic is best for students with stronger geometry and graphics foundations. The visual demo can be impressive, but the research value comes from careful deformation rules and artifact analysis.

Research question

Why can mesh skinning not be directly applied to Gaussians?

The student needs to explain how center, local frame, normal, and anisotropic scale jointly affect rendering quality during animation.

Contribution

Gaussian-aware deformation

Potential contributions include frame / normal updates, bounded scale updates, and joint artifact reduction compared with center-only LBS.

Animation experiments and paper package

Pose rendering demo, joint artifact crops, temporal stability curves, ablations, failure analysis, and a conference-style paper.

Research Position

Core problem

Traditional skinning works on mesh vertices, while Gaussian assets include local frame, anisotropic scale, and appearance.

If these attributes are not updated correctly, splat support regions can become inconsistent with the new pose, causing artifacts around joints.

Outcome

What the project can produce

Research Details

Core technical components

The method focuses on center motion, orientation updates, and scale-aware deformation.

Center-only LBS Baseline

Move only the Gaussian centers to expose limitations of the simplest approach.

Frame / Normal Update

Apply blended rotations to local frames and normals so local orientation follows bones.

Scale-aware Deformation

Control anisotropic scale updates to reduce joint-area stretching artifacts.
